1. Foundational Commitment to Patron Confidentiality

2. Direct Scope of Collected Client Information

During private salon appointments, digital consultation scheduling, or bespoke clutch inquiries via telephone at +1-888-777-5845, we collect necessary client contact records including full name, verified shipping addresses, telephone numbers, and email coordinates. Furthermore, for customized bespoke clutches, our artisans maintain secure dimensional archives documenting personalized monogram initials, bespoke leather dye selections, and hand-stitched thread specifications. 3. Digital Protocols and Encrypted Processing Architecture

Our digital web architecture utilizes state-of-the-art TLS 1.3 cryptographic protocols and transport-layer encryption to secure every interaction conducted through cordovanclutch.com. When patrons schedule an appointment or transmit commission payments, payment credentialing is processed directly through PCI-DSS Level 1 compliant financial gateways with tokenized verification. We maintain a permanent institutional policy never to sell, license, lease, or disseminate patron identifying information to commercial marketing conglomerates, behavioral trackers, or data aggregation platforms. Every third-party logistical partner operates under binding non-disclosure agreements mirroring our high privacy standards.

Regardless of whether you reside in North America, the European Union, the United Kingdom, or the Asia-Pacific region, Cordovan Clutch Atelier extends uniform data autonomy rights across our entire international patronage.
