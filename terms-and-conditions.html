1. Legal Framework and Contractual Agreement

These Terms and Conditions of Bespoke Commission represent a legally binding agreement between Cordovan Clutch Atelier, situated at 181 Mercer Street, New York, NY 10012, United States, and any individual patron entering into consultation, placing a custom commission, or purchasing finished goods. By confirming an order or remitting an atelier deposit, the patron explicitly covenants to be governed by the handcraft timelines, material tolerances, and intellectual property provisions codified within this foundational operational charter.

2. Bespoke Commissioning Protocol and Deposit Obligations

Because every evening clutch, envelope folio, and framed minaudière requires the dedicated procurement of rare equine shell cordovan leather and weeks of master hand saddle stitching, all bespoke commissions require an advance non-refundable deposit of fifty percent prior to hide skiving. The remaining balance, including insured specialized courier fees and applicable state taxes, becomes fully due upon photographic presentation of the completed heirloom piece prior to final dispatch from our Mercer Street workshop facility.

3. Natural Material Variations and Artisanal Authenticity

Equine shell cordovan is an organic, biological corium membrane tanned slowly through traditional six-month oak bark pit immersion without synthetic leveling paints. Patrons acknowledge and appreciate that authentic vegetable-tanned cordovan exhibits subtle natural variations in fiber luster, slight color depth modulations, and characteristic micro-rippling that confirm genuine artisan heritage. These natural organic characteristics do not constitute defects but represent the cherished hallmarks of genuine bespoke leathercraft.

4. Production Timelines and Handcraft Scheduling

Given the exacting nature of two-needle saddle stitching, hot-iron beeswax edge creasing, and individual agate-burnished glazing, production intervals range from eight to fourteen weeks depending on atelier commission volume. While our workshop exerts every reasonable effort to satisfy estimated delivery benchmarks, timeline projections remain subject to the natural drying phases of organic vegetable infusions and specialized hardware machining, and adjustments shall not constitute a breach of this commission contract.

5. Intellectual Property and Architectural Design Rights

All architectural silhouettes, bespoke pattern blueprints, proprietary frame clasp mechanisms, and artistic photographic monographs showcased across cordovanclutch.com remain the exclusive intellectual property of Cordovan Clutch Atelier. The commissioning of an evening clutch conveys physical ownership of the singular leather item but grants no license or entitlement to duplicate, reverse-engineer, commercially mass-produce, or publicly misrepresent our proprietary structural patterns without written authorization.

This commission contract and any disputes arising from atelier transactions shall be governed exclusively by the substantive laws of the State of New York and the United States, without regard to conflicts of law principles. Any legal action or formal dispute resolution proceedings must be instituted within the competent state or federal courts located in New York County.
